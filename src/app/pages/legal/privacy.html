At Adscentials, we believe in privacy by design. We are not in the business of selling your data. This policy outlines our straightforward approach to handling your information.

1. No Tracking

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics pixels to follow you around the internet. Your activity on Adscentials is your business.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum data required to provide our service:

  • Account Info: Your email address and basic profile details to create and secure your account.
  • Payment Info: All payments are processed securely via Stripe. We do not store your credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Content: The ad slots, scripts, and media you upload to the platform.

3. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Facilitating transactions between creators and sponsors.
  • Improving the functionality of the Adscentials platform.
  • Communicating strictly necessary updates about your account or orders.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with essential service providers (like Stripe for payments and Firebase for hosting) to keep the app running.
